    Oriental Pearl Tower (东方明珠), Shanghai's iconic tower

    The Oriental Pearl Radio and TV Tower stands in Pudong, Shanghai, and rises 468 meters. It is famous for its pink spheres supported by three slanted columns. This page shows an interactive 3D model of it: orbit and zoom the model, explode components such as the antenna, capsule, upper sphere, columns and core, lower sphere, and plaza, adjust the sun direction, and view the night mode.
